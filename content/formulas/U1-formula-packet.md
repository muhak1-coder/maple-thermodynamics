# Unit 1 formula and unit packet

public-student | All levels | This is the Unit 1 subset; the full-curriculum packet remains pending.

## F01 - Celsius to Kelvin
T_K=T_C+273.15

Units: T_K: K; T_C: °C

Use: An absolute reading, not a temperature change.

Mini example: 20.0 °C → 293.15 K before rounding.

## F02 - Kelvin to Celsius
T_C=T_K−273.15

Units: T_K: K; T_C: °C

Use: Convert a Kelvin reading.

Mini example: 300.00 K → 26.85 °C.

## F03 - Celsius to Fahrenheit
T_F=(9/5)T_C+32

Units: T_F: °F; T_C: °C

Use: Convert a reading.

Mini example: 5 °C → 41 °F.

## F04 - Fahrenheit to Celsius
T_C=(T_F−32)×5/9

Units: T_F: °F; T_C: °C

Use: Subtract the offset before rescaling.

Mini example: 59 °F → 15 °C.

## F05 - Temperature changes
ΔT=T_final−T_initial; ΔT_K=ΔT_C; ΔT_F=1.8ΔT_C

Units: ΔT_C: °C change; ΔT_K: K change; ΔT_F: °F change

Use: Never add 273.15 or 32 to a change.

Mini example: A +10 °C rise is +10 K and +18 °F.

## F06 - Density
ρ=m/V

Units: ρ: g/mL or kg/m³; m: g or kg; V: mL or m³

Use: Match the mass and volume units to the requested density unit.

Mini example: 40 g/50 mL=0.80 g/mL.

## F07 - Energy at constant power
E=Pt

Units: E: J; P: W (J/s); t: s

Use: Only if the stated transfer power is constant; supplied energy need not all reach the water.

Mini example: 2 W×30 s=60 J.

## F08 - Energy conversions
1 kJ=1000 J; 1 cal=4.184 J; 1 kcal=1 Cal=4184 J; 1 Wh=3600 J

Units: cal: thermochemical small calorie; Cal: food Calorie; Wh: watt-hour

Use: Specify calorie convention and capitalize carefully.

Mini example: 0.50 kcal=2.092 kJ before rounding.
