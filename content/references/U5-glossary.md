# Unit 5 glossary

public-student

## Absolute scale
Temperature scale referenced to absolute zero; use Kelvin for thermodynamic ratios.

## Caloric theory
Historical model treating heat as a conserved substance.

## Calorimetry
Measurement using thermal energy accounting.

## Chronology
Order of events through time.

## Energy quantum
Discrete energy element in a specified physical model.

## Entropy
State property in J/K used in second-law reasoning.

## Heat capacity
Sample coefficient in J/K; generally depends on mass.

## Specific heat capacity
Material coefficient in J/(kg·K) under specified conditions.

## Latent heat
Energy transfer associated with a phase change rather than a temperature rise.

## Macrostate
Coarse bulk description of a system.

## Microstate
Detailed microscopic state within a specified model.

## Multiplicity
Number of compatible microscopic arrangements.

## Primary source
Evidence originating with the investigator or period.

## Reversible process
Ideal limit that can be undone without net effects on system and surroundings.

## Spectrum
Distribution of radiation across frequencies or wavelengths.

## Statistical model
Description using populations and probabilities rather than every trajectory.

## Thermal reservoir
Ideal body maintaining nearly constant temperature during heat exchange.

## Thought experiment
Hypothetical physical scenario used to examine reasoning.

## Frequency
Oscillations per second, measured in Hz.

## Refinement
A later improvement or correction to an earlier account.
