# Unit 5 review

public-student | All competition levels

## Retrieval routine
Explain each contribution as problem → evidence or reasoning → contribution → significance. Use the timeline for dates, the comparison chart for distinctions, and the equation reference for modern connections. A correct name alone is not a complete scientific explanation.

## Review questions
1. Explain how Black’s work undermines the claim that a thermometer measures all transferred energy.
2. Explain why Kelvin temperatures are required in an engine-limit ratio.
3. Follow the energy from falling weights to a warmer stirred sample.
4. Identify what modern theory retained and corrected in Carnot’s work.
5. Distinguish Clausius’s entropy contribution from Boltzmann’s.
6. Explain why Maxwell’s distribution does not assign a fixed speed to every molecule.
7. State what was new about Planck’s radiation contribution without attributing all later quantum theory to him.
8. Give one documented historical relationship and one clearly labeled conceptual connection.

## Readiness
Correct confusion about energy versus entropy, heat versus work, dates versus recognition dates, and historical versus conceptual relationships before proceeding. The next unit treats detailed radiation and the third law as State/National content. History remains all-level.
